01 About

I'm a full stack software developer and technology leader who takes products from concept to live deployment — launching mobile apps, optimizing distributed microservices for real-time performance, and leading teams that turn ideas into production-ready solutions quickly and reliably, including my current role building full stack features at Maverick Games.

Day to day, I build with Go, TypeScript, JavaScript, Vue.js, and PostgreSQL — owning features end to end, from the database schema through the API to the interface on top. I've led development teams through launches and production incidents, keeping delivery moving when it matters.

Outside product work, I enjoy the deep technical end of the stack — my side projects include eBPF-based security policy generation and machine learning intrusion detection, both born from a habit of asking how systems work at the lowest level.
